ONLY WHAT YOU NEED

Rules that save the day.

01 · LUGGAGE

Station lockers, not Airbnb.

Use Nagasaki Station lockers first. Inasa/West side has extra-large lockers. Retrieve bags after Shianbashi.

02 · CUT ORDER

Late? Cut whole branches.

Cut Shofukuji → Kiyomizu → extra Temple Row → Sanno. If badly behind, cut the whole Ryoma branch. Never cut museum, Dejima, Glover, sunset or Inasa.

03 · FOOD

Chinatown = speed lunch.

Champon, sara udon or kakuni manju. If a famous restaurant has a line, eat somewhere nearby instead.

04 · WEATHER

Clouds change viewpoints.

If Nabekanmuri is socked in, stay longer at Glover. Check Inasa visibility before paying for the ascent.

05 · CAMERA

Save Fuji battery for the money shots.

Ryoma hills, Glover/harbor, Nabekanmuri sunset and Inasa night panorama.

06 · TOMORROW

7–8 AM train.

Airbnb: 7-18 Gotōmachi. Pack at night. Leave roughly 25–30 minutes before the train.
